Install on your Pixel

  1. Open this page on your phone and tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded APK. If prompted, allow your browser or Files app to install unknown apps, then tap Install.
  3. Open Sunward, add your sunscreen, and enable notifications and precise timing in More → Reminder settings.
